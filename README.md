# 📊 Reading App Funnel, Retention & Monetization Analysis

An end-to-end product analytics project analyzing user behavior, engagement, retention, and revenue performance for a newly launched reading application (Wattpad-style platform).

---

## 🎯 Project Objective
To understand user journey performance across the product lifecycle and identify:
- Funnel drop-off points  
- Engagement and retention drivers  
- Monetization efficiency  
- Revenue growth opportunities  

---

## 🧩 Dataset Overview
The dataset simulates user activity and includes:
- User signups and onboarding stages  
- Reading behavior and engagement events  
- Retention metrics (D1, D7, D30)  
- Revenue and payment activity  
- User attributes (device, source, country)

---

## 📊 Dashboard Pages

### 1️⃣ Executive Summary
- High-level funnel performance
- Key KPIs: Total Users, Engaged Users, Paid Users, Revenue
- Funnel visualization and major drop-off points

### 2️⃣ Funnel Deep Dive
- Stage-wise conversion analysis
- Drop-off percentage by funnel stage
- Conversion rate by device
- User segmentation filters

### 3️⃣ Retention & Engagement Analysis
- Retention trends by days since signup
- Cohort-style retention (D1, D7, D30)
- Engagement depth analysis
- Device and country-level engagement

### 4️⃣ Monetization & Growth Insights
- ARPU and revenue KPIs
- Revenue trends over time
- Revenue by device, source, and signup cohort
- Paid conversion analysis
- Push notification impact analysis

---

## 🧠 Key Insights
- Strong onboarding and early content engagement
- Significant drop-off at monetization stage
- Android and organic users drive majority of engagement and revenue
- Early active users show higher retention and paid conversion likelihood
